Our Mission

Real problems. Real impact.

AI Club runs student-led service projects that use AI tools to help the school and local organizations solve everyday challenges, while building members' digital leadership skills along the way. Rather than simply learning about AI, members work with clubs, teachers and departments across Stamford American to find a real need, decide whether AI is the right tool, take action, and measure the impact.

What We Do

Five ways we work alongside you

Bring us a problem from your club, your project, or your own curiosity — we will help you find the right tool and the right approach.

01

AI Help Desk

Bring us a problem or idea and we'll help you figure out whether AI can help, which school-approved tools to use, and how to use them responsibly.

02

AI Workshops

Hands-on sessions where students learn practical AI skills together, from prompting fundamentals to building real things.

03

AI Tool Guides

Clear, school-appropriate guides to the tools worth knowing — what each one is good at, and where it falls short.

04

AI Projects

We partner with clubs and students to build AI-supported projects that solve genuine problems around the school. You stay in charge; AI just helps.

05

Responsible AI

Guidance on using AI honestly and safely, so your work stays your own and school policy is always respected.

Responsible AI Pledge

The line we will not cross

“We teach students how to use AI.
We do not use AI to do students' work for them.”

Our guiding principle

To keep that promise concrete, here is what the Club will not do:

  • ✕Complete assignments, essays, or homework for students.
  • ✕Help anyone pass AI-generated work off as their own.
  • ✕Upload confidential or personal information to public AI tools.
  • ✕Bypass or work around school policy on AI use.
  • ✕Trust AI output without checking it against real sources.
